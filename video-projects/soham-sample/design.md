# Sample video design
Concept: an expanding botanical circle gives the viewer room to pause, then makes room for the next step.
Palette: ivory #f4f0e7, forest #173f36, clay #ac593d, sage #cbd6c7.
Typography: Playfair Display 400 for the focal headline; Lato 400 and 700 for supporting text. Both embed through HyperFrames.
Focal element: large left-aligned headline. Edge anchors: SohamYoga upper left and SAMPLE FILM lower left. Supporting detail: one sentence per scene. Background: a large sage disc with nested botanical arcs on the right. Three composed scenes with a calm titlecard entrance and clean fade exit.
