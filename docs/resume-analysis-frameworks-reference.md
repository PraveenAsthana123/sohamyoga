## 4. Resume positioning check

10-15 second recruiter scan should answer: who are you, what seniority, what specialty
(AI Architect / EA / Eng Leader / Strategy Leader), what industries, what scale, what business
results, why you over another 20-year candidate. Target positioning score: 9/10+. One dominant
identity beats a scattered "DBA + Developer + SAP + Cloud + Data + AI + Manager + Researcher" list.

## 5. Career Journey Map

Progression to show: Foundation → Enterprise Applications → Integration/Data → Cloud → Enterprise
Architecture → AI/ML → GenAI → Agentic AI → AI Strategy/Leadership. Track: promotions, increasing
responsibility/architecture ownership/team size/budget/business criticality/executive interaction,
technology evolution, industry/geographic expansion.

## 8. Project Scale Analysis

Capture $ budget, cloud spend, data volume, users, transactions/day, models, APIs, applications,
microservices, countries, business units, vendors, team size, production environments per major
project. Weak: "Led enterprise AI implementation." Strong: "Architected and led a $4M enterprise
AI modernization program supporting 12 business units, 2,500+ users and a 14-member team."

## 37. STAR / CAR / PISTAL / Leadership Altitude / Evidence Pyramid / Final Scorecard

- **STAR**: Situation, Task, Action, Result — bullets should emphasize A+R.
- **CAR**: Challenge → Action → Result (more concise executive variant).
- **PISTAL** (custom framework): **P**roblem/Purpose, **I**mpact, **S**cale, **T**echnology,
  **A**uthority, **L**eadership — strong accomplishments hit 4-6 of these 6 dimensions.
- **Leadership Altitude** (L1-L6): Individual Contributor → Technical Lead → Architect → Manager
  → Director → Executive. Too many L1/L2 bullets weakens Director-track positioning even if
  technically impressive.
- **Resume Evidence Pyramid** (6 layers, bottom to top): Technology → Delivery → Architecture →
  Organizational Scale → Executive Leadership → Business Impact. Common mistake: 70% of resume
  space spent at Level 1 (Technology) when Director/Principal roles need more weight at Levels 4-6.
- **Final Scorecard (100 points)**: Executive positioning (8), Career progression (5), Technical
  depth (8), Architecture breadth (8), AI/GenAI credibility (8), Project/program scale (7),
  Financial/budget ownership (6), Team leadership (6), Executive/C-suite exposure (5), Global
  exposure (4), Industry/domain expertise (4), Business impact (8), Quantification (5),
  Governance/risk/security (5), Transformation leadership (4), Innovation/thought leadership (3),
  ATS/JD alignment (3), Resume readability/story (3). **90-100** = exceptional Principal/Director
  positioning, **80-89** = strong senior candidate, **70-79** = good experience but positioning
  gaps, **60-69** = resume significantly undersells experience, **<60** = major restructuring
  needed.
